How it works

Four steps from inquiry to treatment

Seeking care across borders does not have to be complicated. We guide each step so you can focus on your health.

1

Medical inquiry and case review

Share your medical reports and tell us what you need. We check the documents so your case is ready to send to hospitals.

2

Hospital and doctor matching

We send your case to suitable hospitals and help you compare the options that come back.

3

Appointment and treatment coordination

Once you decide, we arrange the appointment and keep communication with the hospital clear.

4

Medical travel and care support

We help you plan the trip and stay in touch during treatment and after you return home.
