Structured access to Indonesia's outbound patient market

Eagle Go Health is an Indonesia-based cross-border healthcare coordination and market access company. We help international hospitals receive Indonesian patients through structured, compliant and transparent referral coordination.

The market

One of Southeast Asia's largest outbound healthcare markets

Patient mobility is driven by rising health awareness, a growing upper middle income segment and gaps in domestic specialization. Malaysia and Singapore remain the main destinations, while Thailand, India and advanced markets serve cost-sensitive or complex cases.

Our institutional function

A coordination bridge, strictly non-clinical

Informal referrals mean limited visibility, inconsistent documents and unpredictable conversions. We work as a structured bridge between international institutions and Indonesian patients, with defined workflows and ethical engagement standards.

  • Pre-case coordination and documentation alignment
  • Structured communication flow management
  • Referral pathway standardization
  • Distribution channel management within defined governance frameworks

We do not provide medical advice or treatment decisions. Clinical authority stays with the hospital.

Operating model

Our referral framework

Every case moves through the same five stages, so partner hospitals receive structured and reliable referrals.

  1. Market engagement and lead qualification
  2. Pre-case information alignment
  3. Institutional referral coordination
  4. Structured communication management
  5. Post-referral continuity support
Market access and distribution

Two layers that reach patients across Indonesia

We combine digital channels with a network of sub-agents. The digital layer identifies interest and screens cases. Sub-agents handle local intake, first document collection and structured handovers under clear operational guidelines.
