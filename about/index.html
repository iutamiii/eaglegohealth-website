About Eagle Go

Helping patients reach care they cannot find at home

Many patients face limited access to the facilities, technology or specialist doctors they need in their home country. That often leaves families confused and worried when further treatment is needed.

What we do

A medical tourism facilitator you can rely on

Eagle Go helps patients access trusted and technologically advanced international hospitals. We support you in finding the right hospital, coordinating the medical care and staying with you during treatment, so every step is clearer.

Our role is non-clinical. We do not give medical advice or make treatment decisions.

Meet our CEO

Led by Indira Utami

Indira Utami has long been involved in medical tourism through her initiative, Berobat ke Luar Negeri, assisting and referring many patients to receive treatment in Malaysia. That experience is the foundation of Eagle Go's responsible, patient-centered approach.

She oversees the development of cross-border healthcare partnerships, building on her experience connecting Indonesian audiences with global institutions across several sectors.
